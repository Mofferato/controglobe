## What the Shorts take, made our own (cgvideo/shorts_engine)

1. Force comparison as two arrays of soldier glyphs on a darkened map, one glyph per unit of strength, with the numbers
   beneath, falling as the battle goes; in every era's own glyph (legionary, knight, musketeer, rifleman, division
   counter, warhead, drone).
2. The date to the day in battle and by years between, in the corner, always.
3. Encirclement rings, sieges as rings of glowing beads, fleets as swarms of ship silhouettes.
4. A glowing front line between the sides; the map's own colours turned down while the war is on.
5. The cost written along the front at the end of a war.
