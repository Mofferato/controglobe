# Alternate History of Texas (in place of Saudi Arabia): the phases

**The Kingdom of Solms-America, from the Turquoise Road to the Aufbruch, in eight phases.**

This is the special rendition of the Controglobe series for North America. The Arabia video told
2,500 years in one cut, one map a year. This one is told in **phases**: each phase is a video of
its own (12 to 16 minutes), like a "Part 1" in the war-map genre, so that every war of every era
can be simulated on the map in full, with the armies, their numbers and their fronts. Each phase wears the look of the strategy game
that owns its era, redrawn from scratch as our own interface: nothing from any game, film or
other video is reused, only the conventions a viewer already reads at a glance.

The ground is fixed: the basins of the Kansas, the Arkansas, the Red, the Trinity, the Brazos
and the Rio Grande, the Staked Plain, the Sierra Madre and the Sonoran slope. Texas and its
neighbours carry the history of Saudi Arabia (primary) and of the Middle East around it, while
every name, people, faith and frontier stays American in the setting's sense. See
`../../../kingdom-of-solms-america.html` and the Timeline's master key.

## What every phase has (the series' grammar)

These come from the genre's best war-map videos, studied privately (keyframes, chapters, pacing:
`integrations/reference/study_reference.py`), and are rebuilt here larger, clearer and in our own
art. A phase is not finished until it has all of them.

1. **A cold open on the 3D globe.** Blender renders the Earth with ETOPO 2022 relief, the phase's
   map on the continent, an atmosphere and a sunrise, and dives to the kingdom's ground. The host,
   the Solms-America countryball, says one line.
2. **The premise in twenty seconds.** Texas carries Saudi Arabia's role on its own ground; the
   twins in italic (Dreifurt = Riyadh, Paquimé = Makkah, Chukson = Madinah, Waimas = Jeddah;
   the Gulf of California = the Red Sea). The credit to A More Fractured Union is on screen
   whenever the continent is.
3. **A 2.5D map with true relief.** A terrain mesh from ETOPO, textured with Natural Earth II's
   land cover and shaded in QGIS, graded in GIMP for the phase's look; the camera tilts, pans and
   turns, and mountains rise when it does.
4. **Translations and transliterations everywhere a name appears.** English first; then the
   kingdom's own American (the German of the Crossing) where it has a form; the native name
   where a real one exists (O'odham *Cuk Ṣon* for Chukson, Caddo *táyshaʔ* for "friend");
   the setting's script (the Turquoise syllabary of Phase I, the Speakers' hand of Phase II, the
   blackletter of the Crossing ...) with its romanisation; and the Arabic of the encyclopedia's
   second edition, spelt by `ar/GLOSSARY.md` (never the Islamic words for the Path's things).
5. **War simulations with the numbers on the map.** Army counters that move along real valleys and
   passes and lose strength as they go; giant glowing strength numbers for each side, set along
   the front and turned to its line; fronts that advance; arrows that grow; battles that flash,
   shake the camera and tick down casualties; sieges with a progress ring; fleets in formation with
   their counts; attrition marks for thirst and fever; a day, week or season counter that ticks
   while the war runs; and the war's losses on a results card when it ends.
6. **Countryballs with dialogue.** Each polity is a ball in its own flag, with a personality that
   holds across phases (the host ball, Solms-America, in its broad felt hat, frames every phase).
   They appear as cards beside the fronts, as the genre's leader portraits do, and speak in
   bubbles that type themselves out. Humour is dry and never mocks a real people or faith.
7. **The visual identity of the land.** City vignettes drawn in the phase's style (the T-shaped
   doors and macaw pens of Paquimé; Dreifurt's blockhouse; the refinery skyline of Neches), the
   dress, the food, the scripts, the banners, the architecture of each age.
8. **The Arabia video's infobox, in the phase's skin.** Year and era; the flag; the head of state
   when the canon names one; the leading powers and their areas; events with their years; the
   largest towns. It slides away for wars and returns after.
9. **Uplifting music.** A score made in code (`cgsolms/score.py`), bright and forward-moving: major
   and mixolydian modes, open fifths, rising lines, drums that drive rather than menace; the wars
   heroic, never eerie. The user's own music in `assets/audio/solms/` replaces any cue.
10. **The Discord.** A short lower-third half way through, and an end card where the cast asks the
    viewer to help draw the next phase: `https://discord.gg/XYZXVFjQUj`.

## Phase I: The Turquoise Road (500 BCE - 603 CE)

*Marble & Bronze.* Cinzel capitals on veined marble, bronze rims, a laurel at the corners; army
plaques shaped like shields, with the polity's colour, its banner and the strength in thousands
(`11.5K`); battles as crossed spears in a ring that fills with the fight's progress; the map
painted warm, deserts ochre, grass olive, the sea a deep teal. The date reads in years
(`26 BCE`), in seasons during a war (`Spring, 26 BCE`), and in small type in the years before
the Migration from which the Path will count (`643 before the Migration`).

**Carries:** pre-Islamic Arabia. The Turquoise Road is the incense road; Paquimé the caravan city
at its crossing (Makkah); the canal states of the Gila the irrigated south (Saba and Himyar, and
the Marib dam); the Great Houses of the San Juan the Nabataeans and Palmyra; the City of the Gods
in the Valley of Mexico Rome, with Egypt, holding the rim of the kingdom's ground but never its
heart; the Fire-Keepers of the eastern woods Persia, their homeland in the Appalachians (the
Zagros) and their capital on the Great Bottom of the Mississippi, beside where Cahokia will rise
(Ctesiphon beside Baghdad); the Caddo League and the Jumano the Lakhmids and the Ghassanids, the
two empires' clients; the Nahua of the far west, who keep *teoyotl* and the memory of Aztlan, the
Jews of late antiquity.

**Beats** (canon: the page; proposed: new for this phase, see `../phase1/canon.md`):

1. Cold open and premise; the land in 3D: the Staked Plain and the Caprock, the Sierra Madre, the
   Sonoran desert, the Rio Grande, the closed basin of the Casas Grandes, the pine woods.
2. 500 BCE: irrigated valleys and terraced canyons in the west, mound-building farmers in the east,
   and between them the grassland nobody can hold (canon). A bison ball claims it anyway.
3. The Road opens: turquoise from the Cerrillos hills south, macaws, cacao and cotton north
   (canon); the kingdoms that tax it grow (canon in kind, dated as proposed).
4. **War I, the Northern Expedition (26-24 BCE)** (proposed; Aelius Gallus). The City sends 10,000
   of its own, 1,000 auxiliaries of the Great Houses and 500 Nahua north from the frontier
   observatory on the Tropic (Alta Vista) to seize the canal lands. Its guides lead it the long
   way; it besieges Skoaquik for six days and walks home. Seven die in battle and some 7,500 of
   thirst and fever.
5. The long peace: the syllabary is cut on shell (canon, dated as proposed); the lake city of the
   Nahua falls to the City (70) and the Nahua scatter along the Road (proposed; the Second Temple);
   the Great Houses become the City's client (106) (proposed; Arabia Petraea); Paquimé is founded
   where the road from the Rio Grande meets the passes of the Sierra Madre (canon, "about the
   second century").
6. **War II, the Turquoise Queen (268-273)** (proposed; Zenobia). The queen regent of the Great
   Houses takes the Rio Grande towns, Paquimé and the canal lands, then the City's northern march.
   The City answers with 52,000 against her 41,000 on the Conchos and 44,000 against 33,000 in the
   basin of Paquimé, takes the Great Houses after a siege and catches her on the road east.
7. Between two fires: the Fire-Keepers' revival in the east (224); the Caddo League their client,
   the Jumano the City's (proposed); the syllabary in use from the Gulf of California to the
   Arkansas by the fifth century (canon).
8. **War III, the Eastern Expedition (363)** (proposed; Julian's Persian war). 65,000 men and 1,100
   river canoes go up the Red and down the Mississippi to the Great Bottom; they win the field,
   cannot take the capital, lose their general to a spear on the way back, and buy their way home
   with the Red River forts.
9. The two clients' war on the Staked Plain, ending at the Caprock (554), where the Caddo king
   falls (proposed; the Lakhmid-Ghassanid wars and Halima).
10. **War IV, the Year of the Litter (570)** (proposed; the Year of the Elephant). The City's viceroy
    of the canal lands raises a rival shrine, then marches on Paquimé with 24,000 men behind a
    great litter; a spotted fever turns them back at the passes. The Great Headgate on the Salt
    breaks the same year, and the canal towns empty (the Marib dam). Tradition dates the
    Messenger's birth to that year (canon: "remembered only by his title").
11. The City of the Gods burns (598); its northern march falls away. Paquimé, rich and alone, sits in
    the middle of an empty board.
12. Results of the phase (wars, losses, the map of 603); the cliffhanger ("604: a merchant of
    Paquimé begins to preach ..."); the Discord.

**Cast:** Solms-America (the host, from the future), Paquimé, the Canal League, the Great Houses
(and the Turquoise Queen), the Caddo League, the Jumano, the City of the Gods, the Fire-Keepers,
the Nahua, and a bison. Personalities and lines: `../phase1/cast.yaml`.

## Phase II: The Path and the Commonwealth (604 - 1399)

*Parchment & Heraldry.* The map as an illuminated chart on vellum: inked coasts, painted
mountains, rubricated rivers; realms in heraldic tinctures with shields at their capitals;
character cards in round frames with the realm's arms; armies as banners with levies and
men-at-arms counted separately (`4,200 levies · 600 men-at-arms`); war score as a balance bar
under the date; sieges as a tower that fills. Fonts: an uncial for titles, a book hand for text.

**The Messenger is never shown.** He is a light at Paquimé and the four-armed cross of the Mound;
his words are paraphrased in the encyclopedia's register; no ball, no face, no voice. The
Speakers who follow him can be shown as their realm's ball.

**Beats:** the preaching at Paquimé (604); the Migration to Chukson under the Black Hill (617), from
which the Path counts its years; the Messenger's wars with Paquimé (617-625; Badr, Uhud and the
Trench carry them: a small band against a city, with numbers like 313 against 1,000); the return
and the clearing of the Mound of the Cross (625); his death at Chukson (628). The Speakers carry
the Path from the Pacific to the Mississippi and south to the Valley of Mexico within a century
(canon): the battles against the City's successor states and against the Fire-Keepers carry
Yarmouk and Qadisiyyah, with armies of 20,000 to 60,000; the conquest of Mexico (Egypt) and of the
far west; the wars of succession inside the Commonwealth. The seat moves to Cahokia on the
Mississippi (742, canon): the Great Bottom, where the Fire-Keepers had ruled. The country of the
Sanctuaries becomes a land of pilgrimage governed by the families that keep the shrines (canon).
Drought, flood and the wars of its governors empty Cahokia by 1350 (canon), and the phase ends
with no power left that could stop the ships that are coming.

## Phase III: The Crossing (1400 - 1726)

*Gold & Ink.* A clean, bright political map with gilded frames; estates and nation ideas as
cards; regiments counted in thousands with infantry, cavalry and artillery shares; trade routes as
flowing lines (the Turquoise Road becomes the pilgrim road); a month-by-month date.

**Beats:** Europe's tribal confederations cross the Atlantic, pushed by their own wars and the
trade in Europeans (canon); horses, cattle, iron and the diseases of the Old World; the native
population falls by more than half in two centuries (canon, told soberly, as numbers and empty
towns, never as spectacle). Saxons and Angles on the seaboard, Franks on the Mississippi,
Castilians in Mexico, Slavs in the north-west, and on the southern plains the Hessians, Swabians
and Low Saxons (canon): the plains tribes become mounted herders with brands, feuds and elected
captains. The Canadian Empire gathers the rim from the early 1500s (canon; the Ottoman conquest,
with the war against Mexico's old rulers as Marj Dabiq and Ridaniya). The Synod of Chukson (1571)
and the Doctrine of the Two Testimonies (canon). Heinrich, the first Christian Warden, dies (1661);
the schism of the aldermen and the Kindred; Konrad and seventy of his household killed at the fords
of the Gila on 10 October 1680 (canon; Karbala, shown as a small, sad battle, not a spectacle);
the Kindred confession becomes Atlantica's (1701, canon; the Safavids). The interior stays a
country of counties and tribal captaincies, the Solms at Braunfels, the Leiningen on the Arkansas,
the Isenburg on the Brazos (canon), whose feuds the empire's governors manage with subsidies and
punitive columns.

## Phase IV: The House of Solms and the Plain Rule (1727 - 1901)

*Gazette & Brass.* An art-nouveau newspaper frame; fronts drawn as lines with battalions counted
beside them; generals' cards; diplomatic plays as headlines; population and trade as small charts.

**Beats (all canon):** Count Philipp at Braunfels (1727, Founding Day); Johann Eberlin and the Oath
of Braunfels (1744); the County of Braunfels holds the southern plains by 1790; its riders sack the
Kindred shrine at the Gila fords (1802) and hold Paquimé and Chukson (1803-1812); the High Seat calls
its viceroy in Mexico; a Mexican army crosses the Rio Grande (1811), retakes the holy cities and,
after a siege of six months, levels Braunfels (1818); Count Ernst is beheaded at Hochelaga.
Bernhard drives the Mexicans from the Trinity and sits at Dreifurt (1824). The quarrels of Count
Friedrich's sons (after 1865). The Leiningen of Wichita beat the last Solms army in the Cross
Timbers (1891); the family goes into exile in Ludwigsland.

## Phase V: Unification (1902 - 1934)

*Trench & Telegraph.* The full war-map grammar: division counters, fronts, a daily date, aircraft
and armoured lorries, the great glowing numbers.

**Beats (all canon):** 15 January 1902, Karl and forty riders take the Trinity Blockhouse; the
Rotland by 1906; the Ostmark from a Canadian garrison (1913); the Treaty of Neches with Sokoto
(1915); the Great War breaks the Canadian Empire in America; the American Revolt of the Keepers of
Paquimé with Sokotan gold (1916); the Brethren, plains tribesmen settled in farming colonies; Wichita
(1921), the Kansa country and the Staked Plain (1922), Bergland; Paquimé (13 October 1924) and
Waimas (23 December 1925); King of the Sanctuaries (8 January 1926); the Treaty of Waimas (1927);
the Brethren rise (1927) and are broken by machine-guns on lorries at the Salt Fork of the Brazos
(5 April 1929); unification on 4 July 1932; the war with Nuevo León and the Treaty of Camargo
(1934), which fixes the frontier on the rim of the Conchos.

## Phase VI: Oil and the Second Great War (1930 - 1953)

*Steel & Signal.* Hearts of Iron's full war room: air wings and fleets with their counts, supply
lines, factories and the oil itself, barrels a day as a rising meter.

**Beats (all canon):** the salt dome at the mouth of the Neches blows out (1901, under the
Canadians); the Großfeld (3 October 1930); the Arabian concession (29 May 1933) and Amaröl; the
first tanker from the Sabine (1939); the world war on the Gulf; Karl meets President Faisal ar-Rashid
off Sabine Pass (14 February 1945); war on the Kimfumu kya Kongo and Hindustan; the Concert of
Nations and the American League (1945); Aztlan founded and its first war (1948); Karl dies leaving
forty-five sons (9 November 1953).

## Phase VII: Embargo, Revival and the Gulf Wars (1953 - 2005)

*Radar & Telex.* A revitalised Cold War look: the map on a dark plotting table with a sweeping
radar, teletype captions, an oil-price meter that the embargo sends through the roof, satellite
passes, and the war-map grammar in phosphor green and amber.

**Beats (all canon):** Philipp II deposed (1964); OPES founded (1960); slavery abolished (1962);
Ludwig and the October War embargo (1973), the price of crude quadrupled; Ludwig shot (1975); the
Atlantican revolution and the Kindred rising (1979); the seizure of the Mound of the Cross on Good
Friday 1980; the eight-year war between Mississippi and Atlantica (1980-88, a full war simulation);
Mississippi annexes Ludwigsland (1990); more than half a million Arabian troops in the Ostmark and
the Gulf coalition war (1991, a full war simulation with air and sea); the Ground Order (1992);
Neches (1996); the attacks of 11 September 2001 on Jeddah and Umm al-Qura (told as the
encyclopedia tells them: no simulation, no spectacle, the victims counted and named as the canon
names them); the Arabian-led invasion of Mississippi (2003).

## Phase VIII: The Aufbruch (2005 - 2026, and 2030)

*Satellite & Signal.* A revitalised modern look: open-source-analysis maps (control shaded and
dated, front lines with their sources), satellite tiles, thermal drone views for the strikes, a flat
clean interface.

**Beats (all canon):** Albrecht's municipal elections (2005); the risings of 2011; Wilhelm and
Karl Ferdinand (2015); the war in Nuevo León (2015-2022, a full war simulation with the coalition
and the Huastecas, the missiles on Dreifurt and the Großfeld); Aufbruch 2030 (2016); the Karankawa
blockade (2017-2021); women drive (24 June 2018); the Kaschauer killing (2 October 2018, told
soberly); the Großfeld struck (September 2019); relations with Atlantica restored (2023); the
winter of 2023 and the twelve-day war (June 2025) as the kingdom watched them; the quarrel with the
Counties (2025-26); Morgenstadt, the Exposition of 2030 and the world championship of 2034. The
finale: the Arabia video's demographic close rebuilt for the kingdom (population by march, peoples
and confessions, the largest cities), then the whole series on the globe.

## How a phase is made (the production steps)

Each step has a ready prompt below; a session can pick any of them up cold after the master prompt
(`MASTER_PROMPT.md`). The machinery is `video/solms/` (see its README).

| Step | Claude does | You do | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brief | Reads the phase's section here, the canon pages and the last phase's notes | Say what you want changed | The beat list is agreed |
| 2 Canon and script | Writes `phaseN/canon.md` (canon, proposed and inferred, with sources) and `phaseN/script.yaml` (scenes, timings, camera, cards, lines) | Rule on every proposed name, battle and number | No proposed item is unruled |
| 3 Ground | Builds the phase's frame, relief and textures in QGIS and GIMP; traces polities on rivers and watersheds | Look at the plates | The plates are approved |
| 4 Cast and art | Countryballs, flags, scripts and city vignettes for the phase | Approve faces and flags | Every speaker has a ball |
| 5 Wars | Armies, routes, strengths by date, battles and fronts in `script.yaml` | Check the numbers against the canon | Numbers sum; routes follow ground |
| 6 Compose and preview | `python solms.py compose N`, then preview stills of every scene and look at them | Comment on the stills | Nothing collides; everything reads |
| 7 Globe and 3D | `python solms.py blender N`: the cold open, the dives, the hero flyovers | Approve the opening | The 3D cuts match the map |
| 8 Score | `python solms.py score N`: the uplifting score on the cut's cues | Supply music if you prefer | -15 LUFS, peaks under -1 dBFS |
| 9 Render and edit | `python solms.py render N`; the Resolve build script; markers | Run it in Resolve; polish | The cut is approved |
| 10 Thumbnail and publish | Thumbnail (GIMP), title, description, chapters, tags, pinned comment | Upload | Live |
| 11 Encyclopedia | The phase's history into the pages in the same piece of work, both editions, with the Timeline and the glossary; the canon file lists what is proposed | Rule on the proposals; read the diff | Pages and video agree |

### Ready prompts

**1. Brief.** "Read `video/solms/prompts/MASTER_PROMPT.md`, then Phase N in `PHASES.md`, and the
history section of `kingdom-of-solms-america.html` it rests on. List the beats, what is canon,
what you would propose, and the wars you would simulate with their sides and rough numbers."

**2. Canon and script.** "Write `video/solms/phaseN/canon.md` and `script.yaml` for Phase N. Every
proposed person, battle, treaty or number goes in the canon file's table with what it carries
(the real event) and its status. Mark nothing as canon that the pages do not say."

**3. Ground.** "Run `python solms.py ground N`. QGIS projects ETOPO and Natural Earth II onto the
phase's frame and computes the basins; GIMP grades the plates in the phase's look. Show me the
plates and the polity map of every key year."

**4. Cast and art.** "Run `python solms.py cast N` and show me the cast sheet: every ball with its
flag, three expressions and its card, and the city vignettes."

**5. Wars.** "For each war in Phase N, check that every army's route follows passable ground
(valleys, passes, rivers), that strengths fall with the losses the script gives, and that the
results card adds up."

**6. Compose and preview.** "Run `python solms.py compose N` and `python solms.py stills N`, open the
stills and fix what collides, what cannot be read in the time it is on screen, and what looks
unlike the phase's skin."

**7. Globe.** "Run `python solms.py blender N` and show me the first, middle and last frames of each
3D shot."

**8. Score.** "Run `python solms.py score N`. Report loudness and peaks, and the cue list."

**9. Render.** "Run `python solms.py render N` (tell me the time first), then `python solms.py
resolve N`, and give me the paths and the manual polish left."

**10. Publish.** "Run `python solms.py publish N` and `python solms.py thumbnail N`; open the
thumbnail; polish it in GIMP through the MCP server."

**11. Encyclopedia.** "Put Phase N's history into `kingdom-of-solms-america.html`, the Timeline
and their Arabic editions, with new terms in `ar/GLOSSARY.md`, in the same piece of work as the video;
keep `phaseN/canon.md` listing what is proposed, and note it in `video/CLAUDE.md`. Take out together,
from video and pages, anything I strike."
