# Phase I: The Turquoise Road (500 BCE - 603 CE): canon and proposals

What the video of Phase I shows, and on what authority. **canon** rows cite
`kingdom-of-solms-america.html` (KSA) or `timeline-of-the-global-swap.html` (TL). **proposed** rows
are new for this phase: each says which real event it carries, and waits for a ruling. The video
tells them as the story's own, and they have been written into the pages with it, in both editions
(the Solms-America page's turquoise kingdoms and swap key, the Timeline, `ar/GLOSSARY.md`), as the
project's rule wants; anything struck comes out of both.

## Numbers shown on screen

Every number in the war simulations (strengths, losses, canoes, the days of a siege) is proposed,
chosen to stand where the carried event's numbers stand: Gallus's 10,000 Romans with 1,000
Nabataeans and 500 Jews; Julian's 65,000 and his fleet on the Euphrates; Aurelian's and Zenobia's
armies at Immae and Emesa; the traditional figures for Abraha's march. Town populations in the
infobox (the City of the Gods 100,000 at its height, Skoaquik 12,000, Chaco 6,000, Paquimé 4,000 at
its founding, 9,000 by 600) are proposed from the real sites' estimates.
